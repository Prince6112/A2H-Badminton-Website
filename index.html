The court

Built for proper badminton

Fully indoor

No wind and no sun in your eyes, so the shuttle flies true. Play early morning or late evening, whatever the weather.

BWF-certified synthetic mat

A court mat certified by the Badminton World Federation, with consistent grip and bounce under your feet.

Rubberised cushioning

A cushioned layer under the mat absorbs impact, so it's easier on knees and ankles than hard floors.

One court, your hour

A single dedicated court. When you book a slot, the court is yours for the hour.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be a member?

No. The court is open to everyone, and you book by the hour.

How do I book a slot?

WhatsApp or call 72007 96960 with your preferred date and time, and we'll confirm availability. If you can't get through, try our second number, 97515 09580.

How much does it cost?

Please call or WhatsApp us for current hourly rates for badminton and table tennis.

What are your timings?

We're open every day from 6 AM to 10 PM.

What should I wear?

Comfortable sportswear and clean, non-marking indoor sports shoes. These protect the court mat and give you better grip.

Do you provide rackets and shuttles?

Please ask us on WhatsApp when you book, and we'll let you know what's available.

Is coaching available?

Coaching is coming soon. Message us on WhatsApp to register your interest, and we'll contact you when batches open.

Where are you located?
